Model notes

1A / 1B identify the two homologs of chromosome 1. 23A / 23B identify the sex pair: X/X in XX mode, X/Y in XY mode.

1A′ marks the sister copy of 1A. Replication produces 92 chromatids while the chromosome count stays 46. After meiosis II, each product has 23 chromosomes.

Only chromosome 1 and the sex pair resolve DNA segments. Segment numbers and birth origins stay with each exchanged segment; hover over a segment for its origin.

XY crossover is restricted to the outlined terminal PAR1 segment. XX mode shows chromosome segregation, not unequal oocyte division or polar bodies. Shapes, lengths, and positions are schematic.

Changing XX / XY starts a new run.
