Model notes

1A / 1B label the two homologs; 1A′ labels the sister of 1A. The sex pair is numbered 23A / 23B: X/X or X/Y.

One DNA replication is followed by one division. The 46 duplicated chromosomes align individually; homologs do not form tetrads or exchange segments in this model.

At anaphase, sisters separate and become chromosomes: the undivided cell briefly contains 92 chromosomes, with 46 moving toward each pole. Each final daughter has 46 chromosomes and the same starting karyotype.

Gold links show sister cohesion. Arm cohesion is removed during prophase; cohesion near the centromere is released at anaphase. Thin blue fibers depict spindle attachments. The shaded metaphase plate is a projected geometric plane, not a membrane.

Both sister DNA molecules contain one old strand and one new strand. Prime labels are tracing conventions. Detailed segments are shown on chromosome 1 and the sex pair.

Chromosomes remain condensed for visibility. Cell/nuclear envelopes, spindle fibers, lengths, sizes and positions are schematic. Changing XX / XY starts a new run.
